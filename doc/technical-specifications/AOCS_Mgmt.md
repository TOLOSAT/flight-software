## Introduction

The role of the AOCS management task is to execute the satellite attitude control algorithms. These algorithms are supplied by the AOCS team and will perform measurements on sensors and send instructions to the actuators depending on the context. The AOCS management task is therefore to interface between the attitude control algorithms, TAPAS and the AOCS hardware.
